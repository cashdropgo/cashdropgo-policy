CashDrop GO - Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to CashDrop GO ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile game CashDrop GO (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account.
  • Location Data: Real-time GPS location to show nearby virtual banknotes and enable gameplay. Location is only accessed while the app is in use.
  • Camera Data: Camera access for the AR scanning feature. We do not store photos or videos.
  • Usage Data: Game activity including virtual banknotes dropped, collected, and challenge completions.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and advertising identifiers for ad delivery.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core gameplay (map, AR camera, virtual wallet, challenges).
  • To manage your virtual currency balance.
  • To display nearby virtual banknotes based on your location.
  • To show advertisements (for free-tier users).
  • To detect cheating and ensure fair gameplay.

4. Virtual Currency

CashDrop GO uses virtual currency for gameplay purposes only. Virtual currency has no real monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real money. Users receive virtual currency upon registration and through daily bonuses, challenges, and gameplay.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Authentication: Social login. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenStreetMap / CartoDB: Map tiles. No personal data is shared.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely with encryption, JWT authentication, and industry-standard security measures.

7. Children's Privacy

CashDrop GO is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can opt out of marketing and request a copy of your data.

9. Advertising

Free-tier users see Google AdMob ads. Premium subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience.
